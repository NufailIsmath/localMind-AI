# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## 1. Working hours

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may organise their working day flexibly as long as they work 40 hours per week on average.

The Colombo office is open from 07:30 to 19:00 on weekdays. The building is closed on public holidays.

## 2. Annual leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata based on their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March if they have not been used.

Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## 4. Parental leave

The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 6 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## 5. Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the line manager and the Head of People.

Northwind provides a one-time home office stipend of 300 USD for desk, chair and monitor purchases, and a monthly internet allowance of 25 USD.

## 6. Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Conference travel counts against the same budget.

## 7. Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase with an itemised receipt. Claims above 500 USD require pre-approval from a director. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 8. Probation

New employees have a probation period of 3 months. During probation the notice period is 1 week; after probation it is 1 month.
