# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

All staff accounts must use a password manager. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control and the cloud console; hardware security keys are required for administrators.

## Device security

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer backups are classified as Restricted and must never be copied to personal devices. Restricted data may only be accessed from managed devices.

## Incident response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team immediately. The security team will acknowledge a report within 30 minutes during business hours. Customers affected by a confirmed data breach are notified within 72 hours.

## Access reviews

Access rights to production systems are reviewed every quarter by the system owner. Accounts of departing employees are disabled on their last working day.
