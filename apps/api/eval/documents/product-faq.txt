Northwind Cloud Backup - Frequently Asked Questions

What is Northwind Cloud Backup?
Northwind Cloud Backup is an encrypted backup service for small businesses. It backs up laptops, servers and Microsoft 365 mailboxes to Northwind's data centres in Frankfurt and Singapore.

Which plans are available?
There are three plans. The Starter plan costs 9 USD per device per month and includes 250 GB of storage per device. The Business plan costs 19 USD per device per month and includes 1 TB per device plus Microsoft 365 backup. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, unlimited storage and a dedicated support engineer.

How long are backups retained?
Starter keeps daily snapshots for 30 days. Business keeps daily snapshots for 90 days and monthly snapshots for 1 year. Enterprise retention is configurable up to 7 years.

How is my data encrypted?
Data is encrypted on the device with AES-256 before upload and is transferred over TLS 1.3. Customers can optionally manage their own encryption key; if that key is lost, Northwind cannot recover the data.

What is the recovery time objective?
For Business and Enterprise plans, Northwind guarantees that a full device restore can begin within 4 hours of a request. Starter plan restores are best effort.

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every plan can be trialled free for 14 days with up to 3 devices. No credit card is required to start the trial.

What is the uptime commitment?
The Business plan includes a 99.9 percent monthly uptime SLA. The Enterprise plan includes a 99.95 percent SLA with service credits of 10 percent of the monthly fee for each 0.1 percent below the target.

How do I contact support?
Starter customers can use email support with a response time of 1 business day. Business customers get chat and email support with a 4 hour response time. Enterprise customers have 24/7 phone support.

Can I cancel at any time?
Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the billing period. Annual subscriptions receive a 15 percent discount but are non-refundable.
