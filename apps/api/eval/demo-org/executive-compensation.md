# Executive Compensation 2026 — CONFIDENTIAL

Prepared by People Operations for the Executive team and HR only.

## Base salaries

- Chief Executive Officer (CEO): 480,000 USD per year.
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO): 360,000 USD per year.
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO): 350,000 USD per year.

## Bonus structure

Executives are eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 40 percent of base salary, paid in March after the board reviews company results.

## Equity

New executive grants vest over four years with a one-year cliff.
