# IT Support Guide

## Laptop or hardware problems

If your laptop is not working, first restart it and check it is charging. If it still is not working, raise a ticket on the IT helpdesk portal with your asset tag (on the sticker under the laptop) and a short description of the problem.

IT aims to respond to hardware tickets within 4 business hours. If you cannot work at all, ask IT Support for a loaner laptop; loaners can be collected from the IT desk on the 2nd floor.

## Accounts and passwords

Forgotten passwords can be reset at the self-service portal. Locked accounts unlock automatically after 15 minutes; if not, contact IT Support.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report lost or stolen devices to IT Support and the security team within 1 hour, as required by the Information Security Policy.

## New software

Request new software through the helpdesk portal. Software that processes customer data needs approval from the security team before installation.
